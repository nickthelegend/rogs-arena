# Rogs brand art prompts

The cat from rizz-club is replaced by **Rog**, a sly geometric frog. The art was
generated with ChatGPT image generation on 2026-09-13; the prompts are kept here
so the art can be regenerated or extended in the same style.

## Mascot and app icon

Output: `docs/brand/rogs-mascot-source.png` (1254×1254, transparent).
Used as `apps/web/public/icon.png` (512 px) and `apps/web/public/mascot.png` (256 px, header island).

> Create an image: a mascot logo for "Rogs Arena", a fast crypto price prediction game (bet UP or DOWN on 5-minute rounds). The mascot is Rog, a sly, confident frog with a smug half-lidded stare, a street-smart trader. Show only the head, slightly tilted, three-quarter view. It must match an existing brand style: bold flat vector sticker, jagged angular geometric silhouette made of sharp polygon edges (no smooth cartoon curves), solid pure black fill (#000000) with a thick light grey outline (#E1E5E6), eyes drawn only as thin white crescent lines, one thin white smirk line. No gradients, no shading, no text, no other colors. Centered on a transparent background, square 1024x1024, generous padding, still readable at 32px as a favicon.

## Style rules

- Flat black (#000000) shapes with a light grey (#E1E5E6) outline; white line details only.
- Angular, jagged polygon silhouettes, matching the header island and cards.
- Colour comes from the halftone orange → magenta → purple textures behind the art, never from the mascot.
- The wordmark is **ROGS**, set in ABC Gravity Italic (the app's display face).
